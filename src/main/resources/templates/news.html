18 December 2022

15 million leva for school sports grounds

BGN 15 million will be invested by the end of the year in building and equipping new sports grounds at schools and repairing old ones. This is set out in a draft Council of Ministers decision approving a programme for new and repaired sports grounds in state and municipal schools. The document has been published for public consultation.

18 August 2022

Rotary Club donates sports equipment to Vratsa

Rotary Club Vratsa donated four chess tables and four table tennis tables to the Municipality of Vratsa. The new equipment, placed in the park next to the Hristo Botev stadium, was opened by the deputy mayor Tsvetan Stoychkov and the club's president Valentina Angelova.
